Accessibility Statement — Hollywood Mike G

Last updated: 2026-05-09

Michael Gonzalez (sole proprietor) is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We are continuously improving the user experience of hollywoodmikeg.com to apply the relevant accessibility standards.

Conformance Status

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, the standard cited in U.S. Department of Justice ADA Title II guidance and broadly accepted as the benchmark for private-sector websites.

Conformance levels:

  • WCAG 2.1 Level A: ✅ Conformant
  • WCAG 2.1 Level AA: ✅ Conformant (target standard)
  • WCAG 2.1 Level AAA: 🟡 Partially conformant where reasonable

Accessibility Features Implemented

The Site is built with accessibility in mind:

  • Semantic HTML — proper heading hierarchy, landmark regions, ARIA

    • labels where appropriate

  • Color contrast — text meets WCAG AA contrast ratios (4.5:1 for body

    • text, 3:1 for large text)

  • Keyboard navigation — all interactive elements are reachable via

    • keyboard, with visible focus indicators

  • Alternative text — meaningful alt attributes on all images;

    • decorative images marked appropriately

  • Form labels — all form fields have associated labels and clear

    • error messages

  • Reduced motion — animations respect the

    • prefers-reduced-motion browser preference

  • Screen reader compatibility — tested with VoiceOver (macOS/iOS)

    • and NVDA (Windows)

  • Resizable text — content remains readable when zoomed up to 200%
  • No keyboard traps — focus can always be moved with Tab/Shift+Tab

Known Limitations

While we work hard to ensure accessibility, some content may not yet meet WCAG 2.1 AA conformance:

We are continuously auditing the Site for accessibility issues. As of the last review, no significant Level AA conformance gaps have been identified, but we welcome reports from users encountering barriers.

We are actively addressing these issues.

Compatibility

The Site is designed to be compatible with:

  • Recent versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge on desktop
  • Safari and Chrome on iOS / iPadOS
  • Chrome and Firefox on Android
  • Screen readers including VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS, and TalkBack

Feedback and Assistance

  • Your assistive technology (if applicable) — browser, screen reader, etc.

Enforcement

If you are not satisfied with our response to your accessibility feedback, you may file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Justice, Civil Rights Division.

This Accessibility Statement is reviewed and updated at least every 12 months, and any time the Site undergoes significant design changes.
