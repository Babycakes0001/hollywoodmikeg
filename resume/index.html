Mike G.

Actor · Producer · Casting Director
SAG-AFTRA · Los Angeles · 5′11½″ · IMDb nm1943914

For more than two decades, Mike G has been the face you know before you know the name — the bouncer, the heavy, the cholo, the demon. A working character actor whose first national break came on The Tonight Show with Jay Leno in 2003, he's since turned up in studio features (Domino, Crank, The Way Back) and prestige television (Euphoria, 9-1-1: Lone Star, Gentefied, Grimm, The Shield) — and served as Coca-Cola's official “Full Throttle” Low Rider Homie.

Off-camera, he works both sides of the lens as a producer and casting director, with credits spanning independent film, network casting, and specialty crew work.

Also credited as Michael J. Gonzalez
