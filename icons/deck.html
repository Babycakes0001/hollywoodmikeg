The Opportunity

A reason to come back — every weekend.

The collection as a static walkthrough carries the structural flaw of every museum attraction: it is a one-and-done visit. The Weekend Residency closes that gap by layering a recurring live-event calendar onto the artifacts already in the building.

The model rests on an underused truth: a deep roster of beloved genre actors works the convention circuit week to week, and no premium venue has ever offered them a home worthy of their legacy.

That structure is the moat. Treating the people who built these franchises with dignity is the most bookable, repeatable, and defensible program in the market.

The result: a one-time ticket becomes a weekly destination — repeat visits, premium upsells, a fresh national hook every few weeks — with no new permanent build-out.
