The Opportunity

A reason to come back —
every weekend.

Hollywood’s Icons of Darkness holds the world’s largest private collection of authentic screen-used memorabilia — but as a static walkthrough it carries the structural flaw of every museum-style attraction: it is a one-and-done visit. The Weekend Residency closes that gap by layering a recurring live-event calendar onto the artifacts already in the building.

The model rests on an underused truth: a deep roster of beloved genre actors works the convention circuit week to week, and no premium venue has ever offered them a home worthy of their legacy. Icons can — a marquee room on the Strip, beside the very artifacts that made them icons, with a guaranteed fee and 100% of their own autograph and photo revenue kept by the talent.

That structure is the moat. Treating the people who built these franchises with dignity is not sentiment — it is the most bookable, repeatable, and defensible program in the market. It books faster, retains talent, and earns the press and goodwill paid marketing cannot buy.

The result: a one-time ticket becomes a weekly destination — repeat visits, premium upsells, a fresh national hook every few weeks, and casino-driving foot traffic — with no new permanent build-out.

The Sponsor Play — our funding strategy

Anchor-sponsor target: Amazon MGM Studios.

The strategy is to land an anchor sponsor to underwrite the appearance fees, so the calendar is paid for before a ticket sells — turning the one scary line item into near-pure margin. Amazon MGM is the lead target, with a warm door through Kevin Wilson, who runs Domestic Theatrical Distribution there. This is a target profile, not a signed deal — and here’s why it’s a no-brainer for them:

💰
Pocket change. A year of genre-talent fees is a rounding error against an Amazon MGM marketing budget — less than a single trailer.
🍿
It's literally their job. Promote a current Amazon MGM release with its star and real props on the Strip — and when there's no new title, the deep Prime Video horror catalog is the inventory to push.
🔁
The flywheel prints engagement. The exhibit sends people home to stream on Prime; Prime sends subscribers to see the real props; a visitor who's never seen a title goes back to Prime to watch it. Every loop is measurable Prime viewership — the metric Amazon actually buys.
🎬
A marquee Vegas flagship, zero build risk. A branded, press-ready activation on the most-visited entertainment Strip on earth — their stars, their props, their catalog, in a permanent physical moment Vegas money can't easily manufacture. We deliver the room, the talent, and the calendar.

Net to a sponsor like Amazon: cheap, on-strategy, engagement-driving. Net to Icons: the talent calendar fully funded by the sponsor. (Target profile, not a closed deal — Amazon MGM is the lead, with Shudder/AMC and other studio/streamer partners as clean fallbacks if it isn’t the one.)
